UNSAID: an original Wispr Flow India campaign film

A grandfather who has loved his wife for decades but never says it. His granddaughter shows him Wispr Flow, and he starts speaking private notes to himself on WhatsApp. They start as "I love her" and grow over days into specific memories: the braid, her bargaining in the market, the lean early years, her promise that they'd figure it out together. Flow writes down his own words, it doesn't invent them. The film is about the gap between having a feeling and putting it into words, aimed at Indian families where affection is shown more than said.

PSST: Game Jam word-of-mouth growth

At IOTA Club, posters, reels and banners got the Game Jam 19 sign-ups on a tight budget. Instead of explaining more, our team asked juniors "Have you heard of Game Jam? What do you think the prize pool is?" and walked off. Anyone who talked to ten people got a Coke. Within about a week almost everyone knew the name, then we revealed the details on Instagram and college email.

  • Sign-ups: 19 to 167
  • Turnout: up 20% on last year
  • Time to spread the name: about 1 week
  • Cost: 8 Diet Cokes

We never tested which channel drove which sign-ups.

ASK THE FLOOR: inventory app built from user interviews

A customer asks sales "When can my order arrive?" and the answer is scattered across paper, messages and other departments. I visited the plant, mapped who passes what to whom, and interviewed the people who would actually use the app. The plan changed from one shared dashboard to one view per role (owner, sales, production, logistics): same data, different jobs. Still in development, no usage numbers yet.
